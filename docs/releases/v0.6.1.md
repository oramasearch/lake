# v0.6.1

**Released:** 2026-10-24 · **Branch:** `main` · **Previous:** [v0.6.0](v0.6.0.md) (2026-10-24).

A patch so **a blocked account can stop or rename its workspace** ([#1122](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/1122)).
Everything else is v0.6.0. No schema change: it rolls by image alone.

## Fixes

| PR | Change |
|---|---|
| [#1125](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/pull/1125) | The balance gate refused `update_workspace` as a whole, so an account blocked by its balance (every account before its first top-up, including those created before v0.6.0 with a running workspace) could delete its workspace but not stop it. It is now refused only with `desired: running`; stopping or renaming always passes ([`../api/billing.md`](../api/billing.md)) |

## Before it rolls

Nothing new: operator v0.9.5 or later, as for v0.6.0.

## Known issues

Those of [v0.6.0](v0.6.0.md#known-issues), and auto top-up needs each Stripe key's PaymentIntents
permission ([#1123](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/1123), [#1124](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/1124)).
