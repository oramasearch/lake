## Exercised in deployment

From the live end-to-end runs of
[orama-bridge#136](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-bridge/issues/136) (item 1, completed
2026-10-01):

- **Apps and builds.** The ApplicationAgent built and deployed a public app (`e2e-webhook`), a
  lake-protected app (`e2e-hello3`, behind the sign-in handoff) and an app bound to a new Postgres
  service (`e2e-notes`, built in-cluster with `build_app`, pushed to lake's registry and pulled with
  a per-app `pull` token). A note saved through `e2e-notes` survived a reload.
- **Solutions S-1.** Project `e2e-s1f`: three stages, decisions, a balanced 10% holdout, the
  ledger, and webhook delivery to `e2e-webhook`.
- **Experiments.** An ExperimentsAgent proposal, preflighted with `validate_study`, ran 6 trials,
  then compare and promote registered a model.

From the UX review of 2026-10-01 (liquid-bridge `docs/reviews/lake-ux/2026-10-01.md`, epic
[lake#453](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/453)), through the browser, REST and MCP:

- Clerk sign-in and browser sessions; every customer job's pages at 1440 and 390 px.
- A 400-row CSV upload, a project, a pipeline approved and run, run reports loading, a model
  published and called with `predict` from outside the cluster (once `workspace_id` was added,
  [lake#439](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/439)).
- Ask Orama answering and handing off to the ExperimentsAgent; `agent_turn` over REST and MCP.
- `my_audit` and `metering_usage` reads.

Failures the same runs recorded are listed with their issues in the review and in
[`../design/status.md`](../design/status.md); they do not make a capability undeployed.
